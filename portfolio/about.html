About Vinit Gaonkar

Hi, I'm Vinit — a final-year BCA student with a growing passion for building things on the web that are both functional and genuinely enjoyable to use.

My journey into tech started with curiosity about how websites actually work, and it quickly grew into a focus on two areas I love: web development and UI/UX design. I like sitting at the intersection of the two — writing clean, reliable code on one side, and designing interfaces that feel intuitive and effortless on the other. To me, a great website isn't just about what it does, but how it feels to use.

Over the course of my BCA program, I've worked on building web projects that let me apply what I'm learning in the classroom to real, hands-on problems — from structuring a site's front-end to thinking through the small design decisions that shape a user's experience. I'm especially drawn to the process of taking an idea from a rough sketch or wireframe all the way to a polished, working interface.

I'm currently in my final year, using this time to sharpen my skills, take on new challenges, and build a portfolio that reflects both my technical ability and my design sensibility. I'm always looking to learn new tools, frameworks, and design principles that help me grow as a developer and designer.

Looking ahead, I'm excited to bring these skills to real-world projects and teams — whether that's contributing to a product, collaborating with a design team, or building something from the ground up.

Take a look through my work below, and if you'd like to collaborate, have a project in mind, or are hiring, I'd love to hear from you.
